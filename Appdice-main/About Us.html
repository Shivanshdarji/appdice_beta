About Us

At AppDice , we are a small but passionate automation startup with a bold vision: to redefine technology across industries by creating intelligent, human-centered AI solutions.

Our flagship service, ChatAi , features AI chatbots psychologically trained to engage users with empathy and insight, elevating customer interactions to a new level.

Alongside ChatAi, we offer custom automation services tailored to your unique business needs, helping you streamline operations and unlock new efficiencies.

We believe technology should empower people, not replace them. Our mission is to build smart tools that augment human potential and foster meaningful connections.
